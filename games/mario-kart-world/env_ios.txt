# The iPhone app's switches: switch2mac.py ios packs them into the app (dm/env.txt), and switch2mac.py run --phone uses them on the Mac, to try the phone's settings there first.
# On the phone, Documents/env.txt overrides any of them with no rebuild (one KEY=VALUE per line, # starts a comment). docs/ios.md says what each is for.
# Memory statistics every second (memstat.m): Documents/memstat.tsv on the phone
DM_MEMSTAT=memstat.tsv
# The largest block of address space the app can reserve, logged at start: whether LiveContainer passes on the extended virtual addressing entitlement
DM_VA_PROBE=1
# The memory switches measured on the Mac with run --phone (part D: footprint median 11.6 GB over title, race and free roam instead of 12.9, 60 fps, the picture unchanged; docs/ios.md):
# textures deswizzled on the GPU from a copy of their storage, so their pools stay out of the residency set, which iOS wires whole
DM_NVN_DESWZ_GPU=2
# large sparse textures the game draws into get memory only for the 64 KB tiles it maps (Mario Kart World: 10 such textures, 3.1 GB when made whole)
DM_NVN_SPARSE_RT=1
# the terrain's sparse tiles the game unmaps give their memory back, and its tiles over the same physical tile share one Metal tile (heaps 4.4 GB -> 2.0 GB by the end of free roam)
DM_NVN_SPARSE_FREE=1
DM_NVN_SPARSE_SHARE=1
# The guest's memory in a file (hle_svc.c), out of the footprint iOS ends the app at; the kernel writes its pages out when it needs the room. On the Mac (part M, docs/ios.md):
# footprint peak 5.62 GiB instead of 11.58, the picture unchanged, more long frames and 24 s more to the first frame, the Mac short of free memory. Not tried on a phone yet
DM_GUEST_FILE=1
# ... in 20 files of 1 GB instead of one of 20 GB: no stall at the kernel's periodic flush of the file (Mac run C1, 4 Nov: none at its 8 flushes outside loadings; M, with one
# file, had 18 of 54-519 ms). tools/nocopy_cross.m passed on that Mac, and pools across two files were made as usual
DM_GUEST_FILE_CHUNK=1G
# memstat's region walk took about 270 ms a sample with 20 files on the Mac and may hold the task's memory map meanwhile: while samples take over 5 ms, it runs every 10 samples
# (the class columns read - in between), the rest every second. Metal's memory by kind follows it (on the phone, test 4: 270 ms a sample without the walk, 359 with it)
DM_MEMSTAT_WALK_EVERY=10
# Texture views: a texture whose 4 cached views are all taken gets more places, up to 64 (docs/ios.md). On the menus the cache reused no view, so every view was made again,
# and malloc grew about 300 MB on the phone (test 3) until the screen changed
DM_NVN_VC_WAYS=64
# MapPhysicalMemory limited to the application pool, as on the console (docs/ios.md). Without it the alias region grew from 1.25 to 2.25 GB over four races (test 5);
# with it on the Mac (run C3, 4 Nov) it stayed at 1.25 GB with no map refused, and after 8 minutes on the main menu the footprint was 5.54 GB instead of 6.17 (run C2)
DM_MEM_MAP_LIMIT=1
# Pipeline warm-up on again (it was off here, as in part M: about 214 MiB less at the peak and no 70 s of preparation at the start). With DM_MEM_MAP_LIMIT the phone's peak was
# 5404 MiB in test 6 (4 Nov), 828 MiB under the limit, and 78 % of its slow frames were pipelines built while the game played. Now the start prepares the profile's list and this
# phone's own before the first frame, with a progress screen: longest at the first start after an install (Metal's cache is empty then). DM_PSO_MANIFEST=off in Documents/env.txt
